Agent

Connecting…

It proposes a trade only when the news clears every check: a primary source, two independent major outlets, claims that agree, and no credible prediction market against it. Nothing is bought or sold until you approve it here, and the limits in policy.toml agree.

    The agent runs on your own machine

    It reads your holdings and prepares orders for you, so it never runs on the public site. On a Windows computer, one line in PowerShell sets it up and opens this page from there:

    irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ebeirne/atropos/main/scripts/setup-agent.ps1 | iex

    Already set up? Start it with scripts\start-agent.cmd and open http://127.0.0.1:4411/agent.html. The full guide is SETUP-AGENT.md.
