Where the name comes from

Atropos is the Fate who cuts the thread. Prediction markets now trade tens of billions a month and desks read them as signals, but a price is not a probability: on 628 settled Kalshi markets, a contract priced around 40¢ resolved yes only 30% of the time, and a 98¢ sure thing only 84%.

We see our work as cutting that thread: a calibrated, trusted probability for each event, what it does to what you own, and the cheapest hedge that would actually have paid.

01.

What it does

The odds on the next inflation, Fed and jobs reports from Kalshi and Polymarket, what fed-funds futures and Bitcoin options already imply, your holdings read-only from your brokerage, and every CPI and jobs release day since 2021.

02.

How the numbers are made

Every number comes from code: an isotonic fit on settled markets, a basis against the traditional proxy, a trust score from liquidity, spread, freshness and holders, a pair screen for cointegration. Jev checks every sentence against its numbers before you read it.
